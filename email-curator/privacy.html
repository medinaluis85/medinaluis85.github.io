Email Curator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Email Curator is a personal desktop application built and used by a single individual (the developer) to organize his own Gmail mailboxes. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's Google consent, the app accesses the owner's Gmail accounts through three separate OAuth clients with narrow scopes: read-only (gmail.readonly), label management (gmail.labels), and modify (gmail.modify, used only to apply and remove the app's own EmailCurator labels). The app reads message content to classify each message into a filing category.

Where data goes

All processing and storage happen on the owner's own computer. OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Message text is sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) for automated classification by third-party language models; that text is used only to return a filing decision. No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose other than filing the owner's own email.

Data retention

Filing records are kept in a local database on the owner's computer for as long as the app is used. The owner can delete them at any time by removing the app's local data.
